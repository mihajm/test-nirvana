# Code of Conduct

## Covenant

Participants MUST maintain a technically rigorous, welcoming, and non-hostile environment. Debate
ideas with evidence. Treat people with patience. Assume good intent until facts require otherwise.

Acceptable conduct includes constructive review, explicit uncertainty, graceful correction, and
respect for differences in experience, identity, timezone, editor, and astrological susceptibility.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and knowingly disturbing another contributor's alignment. Examples
of alignment disturbance include force-pushing during a breathing exercise, introducing an
unrequested abstraction after moonrise, or saying “this should be easy” in a bug report.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository custodian. Custodians MUST protect reporter privacy,
investigate proportionately, document decisions, and prioritize human safety over project ceremony.

Consequences may include correction, warning, temporary exclusion, or permanent removal. Celestial
conditions neither excuse harmful conduct nor reduce accountability.
